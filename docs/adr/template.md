## Context
What problem are we solving, and what constraints apply? State facts, including the ones that make this hard.

## Options considered
- **Option A:** What it is, what is good about it and what it costs.
- **Option B:** The same. Include "do nothing" when it is a real option.

## Decision
What we will do and why, in the present tense. Include the details someone would otherwise have to rediscover.

## Consequences
- **Good:** What gets better.
- **Bad:** What this costs or leaves unsolved. An ADR with no downside has not looked hard enough.
- **Neutral:** What changes without being better or worse.
